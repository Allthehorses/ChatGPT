01 · The engine

Carbs are the
daily currency.

Across each day8–12g carbohydrate / kg body mass

High-volume, consecutive endurance work calls for the top end of normal endurance fueling.

While riding60–90g carbohydrate / hour

Start early and feed regularly. Waiting for fatigue is already too late.

Why it matters5×opportunities to recover

Miss today’s fuel and it becomes harder to restore glycogen before tomorrow’s ride.

Personalise with body mass, pace, temperature and tolerance. Practice the plan well before the event.

02 · The toolkit

Gels are
the accelerator.

Useful, portable and fast—but not the whole menu.

1

Start around 60–90 min

Have your first gel before the effort catches up with you.

2

Then every 45–60 min

Adjust this around food and sports drink to hit your hourly carb target.

3

Pair with water

Concentrated carbohydrate without enough fluid can upset the stomach.

HERO MOVE Use gels for a quick lift, climbs, late-race fatigue or when solid food stops sounding good. Combine with real food to avoid palate fatigue.

03 · A repeatable rhythm

Eat little.
Often.

−4 to −1 h

Set the base

Choose familiar, carbohydrate-led food: porridge, toast, banana, pancakes. Keep the closer-to-start meal simple and low in fibre/fat.

0–90 min

Ride ahead of hunger

Start fluids early. Begin regular small feeds even while energy feels good.

Every hour

Land 60–90 g carbs

Mix gels, drink mix and easy whole foods. Keep everything accessible from the saddle.

Finish →

Begin tomorrow now

Rehydrate, eat carbohydrate and add protein soon after the ride—then continue to replenish across the evening.

04 · Heat changes the equation

Replace what
sweat takes.

H₂O

300–600 mg sodium / serving

Electrolytes are especially relevant in warm conditions, longer rides and for heavy sweaters. A practical starting point is a product with sodium, then test it in training.

  • Use drink, tablets or powder—whatever you tolerate.
  • Keep water and electrolyte/carbohydrate drinks in the mix.
  • Adjust for heat, sweat rate and how you feel.
